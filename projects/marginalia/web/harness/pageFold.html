Page fold harness. The surface the curl's look is developed on — see docs/marginalia/PAGE_CURL.md. It exists because html2canvas renders the epub.js iframe blank in headless Chromium, so the fold cannot be judged from a real page turn in automation.

Query params: ?theme=paper|sepia|ink|all · &arc=1 (multiplier on curlArcLength) · &w=340&h=480 · &dpr=2 · &only=drag-50 (one state, drawn large) · &back=real|mirror (M27: the leaf's real other side, or the pre-M27 mirror of its front — the comparison SHOW_THROUGH, backOfSheet's lift and sheenScale have to be re-judged against)

Note on single-page mode, which is what this harness draws: one turn advances one page, so the leaf's back and the page revealed beneath it are the same page. That doubling is a real consequence of the 2026-08-03 ruling ("the whole card in single-page mode") and is shown here honestly rather than papered over. In spread mode the back is the far half of the destination spread and no such doubling arises.
