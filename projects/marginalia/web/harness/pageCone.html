The hinge, next to the fold it replaces. Both stages take the same drag. The difference to look for is at the spine, the edge opposite the grab (the gutter in spread mode): the flat sheet slides away from it, the hinged one is bound to it and cannot. See docs/marginalia/PAGE_CURL.md §2d and decisions.md 2026-08-26.

It is a gesture, not a pose — press, drag, release. Grab the paper anywhere and the sheet is held there: at a corner you get the dog-ear, at the middle of the edge you get a crease through your own pinch, and either one fans as the pointer rises. Let go and the sheet finishes the turn on its own — a drag can never reach the end of a bound sheet's turn, because the paper runs out first — swinging about the apex the release froze while its curl relaxes flat. Below the commit threshold it falls back instead. Press again to re-arm.

Watch the far leaf. It stays page 64 for the whole turn and only becomes 66 when the sheet lands on it — 65 on the sheet, 66 on its back, 67 revealed underneath. The reader advances the rendition at grab time and so shows 66 twice; that is the thing this page is showing the fix for.

Query params: ?model=both|flat|cone · &mode=spread|single · &theme=paper|sepia|ink · &state=peel-20|drag-50|edge-pull|top-corner (the same names pageFold.html uses, so the two can be put side by side) · &t=0.6 (a point on the hinged sweep instead — that path is square across, i.e. the far field, and shows no fan) · &anchor=bottomRight|topRight · &w=380&h=540&dpr=2 · &debug=1 (mark the spine) · &fixture=letters (one huge glyph a side instead of prose — the only way to see at a glance whether a face is mirrored, upside down, or the wrong side of the sheet: turning the right leaf should show R upright on the part still lying flat, and A mirrored on the part folded over)

One thing to allow for when judging. The 3D seam's camera is fitted to the viewport, and it is a real lens — so a stage sitting far from the centre of the window is seen more obliquely than the reader's own leaf ever is, and its lifted sheet splays further. Judge the shape side by side, but judge the amount of splay with ?model=cone and the window sized so the stage is near the middle.

What is not yet true of the app. Nothing is wired to the mesh — the reader still turns pages with the flat painter. This page drives PageFold3D directly so the hinge can be judged before that wiring lands. Two consequences to expect rather than report: the flat stage's canvas is sized to the leaf and cannot cross the gutter, so its half of a completed turn ends with the sheet sliding off the page instead of landing on the facing one (that is §2d, not a bug here); and only a next turn is fixtured — prev is the mirror of everything above and is not built.

Driving it under automation. window.__fold exposes the live anchor, pointer, arc and settling, so a screenshot can say which pose it caught. Wait on window.__foldHarnessReady, and re-read the origin (dispatch a resize) after changing anything above the stage — the leaf's viewport origin is cached per frame, so hiding the header moves the stage without the fold hearing.
